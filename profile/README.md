### What we build

**[Ergenza](https://ergenza.com)** is HR and payroll for growing companies. It covers employees, attendance, leave, payroll and self-service in one configurable platform, and runs in any country.

### How we work

- **Config over code.** Rules, statutory values and workflows live in data, not hard-coded branches.
- **Enterprise-grade by default.** Multi-tenant, audited, and secure from day one.
- **Human-led, AI-assisted.** A small team works alongside AI to ship faster without cutting corners.
